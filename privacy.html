1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the AK AUTO ELECTRICAL business information website and to enquiries sent to us using the contact details published here. It should be read alongside our Terms & Conditions and Disclaimer.

This website is an informational presence. It does not currently offer user accounts, an online payment checkout, or a server-side enquiry form.

2. Information we receive

If you contact us by telephone, email, or WhatsApp, we may receive information you choose to provide, such as your name, contact details, and the contents of your enquiry. Please share only information relevant to your question.

When you access a website, the hosting provider may process limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, request time, and security logs to deliver and protect the website. The exact logs and retention periods depend on the hosting provider.

Please do not send passwords, one-time passcodes (OTPs), full payment-card details, or unnecessary identity documents by email, phone, or messaging.

3. How we use information

We may use enquiry information to respond to your message, clarify a business question, communicate about a potential transaction, keep appropriate business correspondence, and protect the website or business from abuse. We do not state or promise any use beyond these purposes.

4. Disclosure and third-party services

We do not sell personal information collected through direct business enquiries. Information may be available to communication or hosting providers where necessary for the service to function, or disclosed when required by applicable law or to protect legal rights and safety.

If you choose to contact us through WhatsApp, your use of that service is also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy terms and settings. External websites linked here have their own policies, which we do not control.

5. Security and retention

We take reasonable care with business communications, but email, phone calls, and third-party messaging services are not risk-free. Do not send sensitive authentication information to us.

Enquiry details are retained only for as long as reasonably needed to answer the enquiry, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, or meet legal requirements. Actual retention depends on the nature of the communication and any applicable obligations.

6. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have provided, request correction of inaccurate contact details, or ask us to stop using information for a particular enquiry where reasonably possible. We may need to retain certain records where the law requires it or where they are reasonably necessary to resolve a matter.

To make a request, contact syedifteqar3@gmail.com. Please do not include passwords or OTPs in your message.

7. Children's privacy

This website is intended for general business enquiries and is not designed specifically for children. Please do not knowingly send personal information about a child unless it is necessary and you are authorised to provide it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when website practices or applicable requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates the current published version. Please review this page periodically if you continue to use the website.
